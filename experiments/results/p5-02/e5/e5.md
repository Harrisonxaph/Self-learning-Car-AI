**In words:** on all three fresh circuits every one of the 300 episodes ended in a crash and **no start of any champion completed a lap**. With every start crashed on both sides, the collision difference is exactly zero with a zero-width interval, so it is `no-clear-difference`; with no start where both champions completed a lap there is no eligible pair for lap time, so that endpoint is `inconclusive` and is not replaced by another measure (the rule's own provision). The report notes that on `hairpin-run` and `hook-chicane` both agents' median progress is below 0.25 lap, and that **a floor effect is a possibility that has not been tested here**; the cause of a weak result is not established. The A5 champions' brake usage (ticks above a brake command of 0.05) was 1,032 of 16,682 ticks on `hairpin-run`, 3,861 of 36,387 on `sweep-hook` and 3,071 of 23,056 on `hook-chicane`; the A4 champions' was exactly 0 on all three, as the design requires.

## What this does and does not say

- **It says** that, for these ten champion pairs on these three circuits, **the study could not detect a lap-time or a collision difference between braking and no braking, because no champion completed a lap and every episode crashed**. That is a null by saturation, not evidence that braking has no effect.
- **It does not say** that learned braking helps, that it does not help, or that the two are equivalent; nothing about other circuits, other training configurations or seeds; nothing about the champions' driving on their training circuit (E4) or on the validation circuits.
- **Limits stated before the run still hold** (protocol section 4): the champions come from one configuration (C1) on one training circuit; the circuits are geometric proxies, designed by the same person who wrote the agents after seeing E3's results (disclosed); the five starts are not independent samples; the intervals are over ten seeds only and pointwise; the champions were chosen by training-circuit fitness, so E5 is not independent of E4's seeds.
- **A possible floor** is exactly the outcome the preregistration listed as possible and said it would report as such, never prevent by editing a circuit; nothing was changed after the look.

## Observations for the owner's decisions (not conclusions)

- **E6 (P5-04) under the plan's default option.** E6 reads A4 and A5 from this record. These champions completed no lap on these circuits, so under the shared-circuit option E6's evolved cohort would have a lap-completion rate of zero on all three, whatever the baselines do. Whether E6 is still worth running on these circuits, on a second batch, or with a different endpoint is decision 1 and 3 of the merged P5-04 plan and is the owner's.
- **Whether E5 should be repeated in some other form** (for example on circuits the champions can partly drive) is a new study with its own preregistration, not a second look: the ledger refuses one.
