## Result

**Metric:** ordered progress, gates per lap (`ordered-progress-gates-per-lap-v1`); predeclared meaningful difference δ = 0.25 lap; decision rule `p5-01-e3-decision-v1`; 10 independent seeds (3001 to 3010); 95% percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples. **A5 − rule**, mean over A5's ten seeds (the rules are deterministic, one value per circuit):

| Circuit | Rule agent | A5 − rule (mean) | 95% interval | A5 higher / tied / rule higher | Label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rounded-rectangle (validation) | A3, A3-nb, A3-nb-same | 0.029 | -0.043 to 0.136 | 1 / 6 / 3 | `no-clear-difference` |
| hairpin-chicane (held-out) | A3, A3-nb, A3-nb-same | -0.094 | -0.122 to -0.061 | 0 / 2 / 8 | `no-clear-difference` |
| long-sweepers (held-out) | A3 | -1.383 | -1.650 to -1.121 | 0 / 0 / 10 | **`rule-higher`** |
| long-sweepers (held-out) | A3-nb | -0.967 | -1.233 to -0.704 | 0 / 0 / 10 | **`rule-higher`** |
| long-sweepers (held-out) | A3-nb-same | 0.450 | 0.183 to 0.713 | 6 / 0 / 4 | **`a5-higher`** |
| technical-narrow (held-out) | A3, A3-nb, A3-nb-same | -0.178 | -0.187 to -0.174 | 0 / 0 / 10 | `no-clear-difference` |

**Reported as (the protocol's wording, per circuit):**
- `long-sweepers`: "the rule reached meaningfully more ordered progress than A5 on this circuit" for A3 and for A3-nb; "A5 reached meaningfully more ordered progress than the rule on this circuit" for A3-nb-same (the rule with the brake masked and the same limits as A5).
- the other three circuits: "no clear difference: a predeclared meaningful advantage was not established on this circuit". This label does **not** mean "no effect" or "equivalent". On `hairpin-chicane` and `technical-narrow` the intervals lie just below zero but the means are within δ of zero, so the preregistered rule gives this label.
- **The four circuits disagree, so no pooled claim and no "evolution beats rules" headline is made** (protocol section 3).

## What this does and does not show

- **Shown:** on these four circuits, with this A3 (a rule written by one person for this simulator, tuned on one training circuit only), this A5 (configuration C1, ten seeds) and this metric, the outcomes above. On three of four circuits no predeclared meaningful advantage was established; on one, two rule variants were far ahead and one brake-masked rule variant was behind A5.
- **Descriptive, not explained.** Nearly every agent crashes early on most circuits: in `analysis.json`, A5 crashed in 10 of 10 episodes on every circuit, and the rules crashed on every circuit except `long-sweepers`, where A3 and A3-nb did not crash (one lap completion each) and A3-nb-same did. **Why** the rules progress further on `long-sweepers`, and why A3, A3-nb and A3-nb-same give identical figures on three circuits, was **not investigated**, and nothing here claims a cause.
- **Not shown:** anything about learning, about generalisation beyond these four circuits, about rule-based driving in general, or about other circuits, tracks, configurations or metrics. A pre-opening guess of mine that both drivers would sit near the floor on the held-out circuits was wrong for `long-sweepers`.
- **Limits (stated before the run, unchanged):** the rule agents are deterministic (the interval is over A5's seeds and does not treat A3 as a sample); A3 was tuned on the training circuit only; ten seeds and four circuits make an interval a guide, not proof; the held-out circuits were designed by the author of the agents from criteria fixed beforehand; the 60 s episode limit may bind (untested); Phase 2's training-track-only result is unchanged and E3 does not revise it.
- **The held-out look is spent for this protocol** (the ledger refuses a second): the three circuits are no longer unseen for any later study that also uses them (P5-04's E6 is the next).
