## Result

**Outcome (`p2-09-e4-decision-v1`): `no-direction`.**
**Reported as:** "no direction established: the interval spans zero".

This is the fourth of the protocol's four labels. It means only that **a predeclared meaningful difference between A5 and A4 was not established, and the direction is uncertain.** It does **not** mean "no effect" or "equivalent": ten seeds and one configuration cannot show that. The interval is narrow and sits close to zero (see below), but the label is the one the preregistered rule gives, and it is reported as it came out.

### The training-track feasibility limitation, carried forward

The braking feasibility check (#116, acceptance A2) was met on the validation track only (+2.217 s against a 0.537 s margin) and **did not show braking on the training oval** (−0.117 s against a 0.515 s margin) for a scripted driver. The protocol said a null or below-threshold E4 result on the oval was a plausible outcome. This result is of that kind, but it **does not show that braking is useless**:
- it is one configuration (C1), one training track and ten seeds;
- the feasibility check was one hand-written driver family on a finite grid, and E4 is about what an evolved network does;
- nothing is changed because of the result: no track, physics, measure or condition. Any redesign needs the owner's approval and a version bump before a fresh study, and is never chosen using held-out results.

## Checking these results

- **Bytes:** `gunzip -c seed-<s>.json.gz | sha256sum` gives the SHA-256 in the Measurements table.
- **Without simulating:** `tests/training/e4Results.test.ts` parses all ten records strictly against the manifest, checks those SHA-256 values and the accounting above, and recomputes `analysis.json` and `report.md` from the records. It takes a few seconds and runs no seed.
- **By simulating:** `npm run e4 -- replay` on a record (about 7 minutes each) and `npm run e4 -- analyse` on all ten (about 77 minutes) from a clean worktree at the frozen commit under Node v24.22.1. Replay and analysis refuse a record from another engine.

## Limitations

- **Scope:** one configuration (C1), one training track and ten seeds. The result is a statement about the training track only. It is not evidence about unseen tracks (E5, in P5-02, needs its own preregistration), and it is not evidence from the feasibility check, which concerned a scripted driver.
- **Validation:** both agents crashed in every validation episode, so the validation contrast says very little, and it decides nothing.
- **The measure:** every champion training episode ended at the 60 s episode limit, so the preregistered progress measure was observed at that horizon only. Whether the limit explains how closely the two agents' progress clusters was not tested (see above).
- **Device evidence:** the runs were headless in Node. This is not device evidence.
- **Nothing here is a claim** that learned braking helps or does not help. P2-09's other gates (AGENTS.md) and the owner's decision on completion are separate (plan step 8).
