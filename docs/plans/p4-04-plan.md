# P4-04 plan: training, validation and held-out track sets

> **Status: plan only, for Codex review and the owner's merge. Implementation needs its own owner authorisation.**
> - **Authorisation:** the owner's "156 is merged, let's do p4 still" (2026-11-11), read as the next Phase 4 step; with the P4-02 plan (#157) awaiting review, this is the next unblocked Phase 4 item (dependencies P4-01 and P2-10 are Complete). Read, as for earlier items, as authorising a **plan only**.
> - **Scope:** the roadmap entry and issue #31: expand the built-in split manifest to at least 2 training, 2 validation and 3 held-out circuits with varied corner geometry; split enforcement tests; recorded content versions and split provenance; a way to select one training circuit per run. **Not** in scope: multi-track training (R-06, approval-gated), evaluating any model on the new circuits (P4-05, E3, E6), the editor (P4-02), Silverstone (P4-06), any physics change, any learning claim.
> - **Lessons carried from P2-10 and the study plans:** the held-out boundary is the integrity of every later generalisation claim, so the new circuits are specified **before** anything is evaluated on them; no model, tuning or selection ever sees them; a held-out circuit changed after a result exists becomes a validation circuit and a new held-out circuit is made (`docs/AI_EVALUATION.md` §5).

## 1. What P4-04 must deliver (ROADMAP, issue #31, AI_EVALUATION §5)

- **Description:** expand the P2-10 split manifest with varied corner geometry. Users select one training circuit per run and evaluate on separate circuits; evolving across several training circuits is the approval-gated R-06.
- **Acceptance:** at least 2 training, 2 validation and 3 held-out tracks; validation and held-out tracks never used by the trainer and held-out tracks never used for tuning or champion selection (**test enforced**); content versions and split provenance recorded.
- **Tests:** unit split enforcement.
- **AI_EVALUATION §5:** held-out circuits "should vary corner geometry (hairpins, chicanes, long sweepers, mirrored layouts and different widths) so that one memorised steering sequence cannot complete them."

## 3. Design

- **Four new circuits, specified by geometric criteria before anything is evaluated.** Each is a closed circuit driven counter-clockwise (the engine's one direction; "mirrored" layouts are therefore **right-hand-dominated sections inside a counter-clockwise lap**, not clockwise circuits), passing `validateTrack`. **These design bounds apply to the new circuits only:** 8 to 14 m wide and 400 to 650 m long (a lap fits the 60 s episode). The existing circuits are unchanged and exempt (`rounded-rectangle` is a 394 m lap).

  | Split | ID | Corner geometry the circuit adds |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | training | `kidney-loop` | no straights: continuously varying curvature, radius from about 25 m to about 90 m, 12 m wide |
  | validation | `s-sweepers` | two long, gentle opposing-curvature sweepers (an S section with a right-hand bend, radius at least 60 m) joined by short straights, 13 m wide |
  | held-out | `long-sweepers` | large-radius arcs (at least 60 m) and one straight of about 100 m, 10 m wide: speed-dependent racing line. **Amended on the owner's approval (2026-11-12, #31): the plan first said about 200 m, which cannot fit the 400 to 650 m lap bound with arcs of at least 60 m (360 degrees of turning at 60 m radius is already 377 m).** |
  | held-out | `technical-narrow` | 8 m wide, alternating left and right kinks and one 135-degree corner: tight, right-hand-heavy sections |

  with the existing `stadium-oval` and `hairpin-chicane` kept as they are, giving **2 training, 2 validation and 3 held-out**. The criteria above are fixed by this plan; the exact geometry is generated from them and not adjusted for any model's performance.
- **A committed, deterministic generator** (`scripts/makeTracks.mjs`, with its specification in `src/data/tracks/specs/`): each circuit is a list of straights and arcs; the generator samples the centre line (arcs every 15 degrees, straights split into pieces of at most 10 m, as for the built-ins), offsets by half the width, places gates on boundary vertices about 25 m apart, puts the start behind gate 0 facing forward, rounds to 3 decimals, runs `validateTrack`, and writes the JSON. **The committed JSON files are the source of truth** (their hashes are pinned in the manifest); the generator exists so the construction is reproducible and reviewable, and a test regenerates the files and requires byte-identical output.
- **Manifest and provenance.** Each manifest entry gains an optional `provenance` object (`introducedBy`, `designCriteria`, `designedBeforeAnyEvaluation: true` for the new circuits, and for the P2-10 circuits their own introduction), recorded next to the existing `version`, `split` and `contentHash`. The existing circuits' files, IDs, versions (each is `"1"`, legitimately shared) and hashes are unchanged, and the manifest's format version is unchanged. **Versions are checked per circuit, not globally:** each entry's `version` must equal the version in its own file and its `contentHash` must match that file; two circuits may share a version string, but IDs, content hashes and geometry hashes must be distinct (no circuit is a copy of another).
- **Enforcement, tested for every circuit.** (a) The trainer refuses every validation and held-out circuit (`assertTrainingTrack`); (b) `createEvaluationSetup` refuses every held-out circuit and every renamed or relabelled copy of one (identical geometry); (c) `heldOutTrack` refuses a non-frozen model for every held-out circuit and a training or validation ID; (d) `selectByValidation` accepts validation scores only; (e) **a source-scan test fixes which modules may call the restricted accessors**: `heldOutTrack` only in `trackSplits.ts`, `heldOutAccess.ts` and tests; `validationTrack` only in the named evaluation modules (agents, feasibility, e4Agents and their callers), so a new module that reached for held-out or validation data would fail a test and need review.
- **Selecting a training circuit per run.** The training page (`?mode=train`) gains a **Training circuit** choice listing the training circuits (default `stadium-oval`), disabled while a run is in progress, recorded in the run's configuration, in saved models and in checkpoints through the track id, version and hash those formats already carry. A checkpoint made on one circuit is refused on another (the existing track check). Validation and held-out circuits are never offered. Multi-circuit training stays R-06.
- **Compatibility with older records (a bounded, tested strategy).** Adding circuits changes the recorded split manifest and its hash, and `parseExperimentRecord` (and the P2-08 and E4 study parsers through `splitManifestHash`) currently require the recorded manifest to equal the **current** one exactly, so without care every pre-expansion record would be rejected. The plan therefore introduces **split-manifest epochs** as an **append-only, committed registry (`SPLIT_EPOCHS`) of explicit circuit-ID lists**: epoch 1 is the original three circuits, and **every change that adds circuits appends exactly one new epoch** (the previous epoch's IDs plus the added ones; an epoch is never redefined). This change appends the epoch with its four new circuits; another addition, such as Silverstone (#159's plan), appends its own epoch whether it lands before or after this one, and an additional circuit cannot be absorbed silently into an existing epoch. `manifestRecord(epoch)` recomputes the manifest record for an epoch **from the current built-in data restricted to that epoch's circuit IDs**, so each historical entry is still checked against today's file and hash (a changed or removed circuit makes the epoch's record differ and is refused). A parser accepts a recorded manifest **only if it equals the record of a known epoch**, and checks the record's training track against **that epoch's** training list; new records are written with the latest epoch. **Corruption and leakage checks are not relaxed:** an unknown manifest, a changed entry, a track outside its recorded epoch's split, or a validation or held-out training track is refused exactly as now. The compatibility claim is reconciled per contract instead of blanket: experiment records and the P2-08 and E4 manifests and records (epoch-aware as above); saved models and checkpoints (they carry track id, version and hash and are checked against the registry, so an added circuit changes nothing); the P5-01 tuning record (training circuit only, unaffected); and the E3 protocol, which will pin its epoch and held-out list explicitly. **Tested on genuine records:** the first commit of the implementation PR adds fixtures **produced by the unchanged code before any manifest change** (an experiment record, a P2-08 and an E4 mechanics record with their manifests, a saved model and a checkpoint), and every later commit must parse and replay them; tests also show that a tampered epoch-1 record, a record claiming a held-out training track and an unknown manifest are still refused.
- **No change to any existing result.** The three existing circuits' files, hashes and IDs are untouched, and the studies and records above keep their meaning through the epoch rule; no existing fixture geometry or version is edited to satisfy a new test.

## 4. What is deliberately not claimed

Adding circuits does not show that any model generalises; it only makes a later test possible. The new held-out circuits are "unseen" only because no result about them has been looked at, which the provenance records and the tests keep true; the designer of the circuits is also the developer of the agents, so the criteria are written down first and the owner and Codex review them. Four hand-specified circuits are a small sample of layouts. No learning claim is made.

## 5. Decisions for the owner (defaults proposed; each routine unless changed)

1. **The four circuits and their criteria** in section 3 (names, widths, lengths, corner geometry), and that the minimum 2 / 2 / 3 is met exactly.
2. **Right-hand-heavy sections instead of mirrored circuits**, because the engine credits one driving direction (a clockwise circuit would earn no checkpoint credit).
3. **A committed deterministic generator** with specs, the JSON files remaining the source of truth.
4. **An optional `provenance` field** in the manifest, with the manifest's format version unchanged.
5. **The Training circuit selector** on the training page, in this item rather than later.
6. **E3 may list all three held-out circuits** in its protocol (a decision for the P5-01 protocol step; it changes nothing here).
7. **The source-scan allowlist** for `heldOutTrack` and `validationTrack` as the "test enforced" mechanism.

## 6. Tests

- **Unit (manifest and tracks):** at least 2 training, 2 validation and 3 held-out; every circuit passes `validateTrack` with no errors; IDs, content hashes and geometry hashes distinct (no circuit is a copy of another), and each entry's version and content hash match its own file (**shared version strings such as the existing `"1"` are allowed**); the **new circuits only** satisfy the new design bounds (width 8 to 14 m, lap 400 to 650 m): the existing circuits are exempt and unchanged (the validation circuit `rounded-rectangle` is 394 m, below the new minimum, and stays as it is); every entry has non-empty provenance and the new held-out entries say `designedBeforeAnyEvaluation: true`; a scripted centre-line follower (the existing geometry check, which is not a trained or tuned model) completes a lap on **every** circuit, so each is drivable; the generator regenerates the committed files byte for byte.
- **Compatibility:** the genuine pre-expansion fixtures (section 3) parse and replay through the epoch-aware parsers; the refusals listed in section 3 still hold; new records carry the latest epoch.
- **Unit (split enforcement), for every circuit:** the trainer refuses validation and held-out circuits; evaluation refuses held-out circuits, renamed copies and edited-ID copies; `heldOutTrack` refuses non-frozen models and wrong-split IDs; `selectByValidation` accepts validation scores only; a held-out circuit cannot be reached through the training page's selector or any saved-model registry (`tracksIn('training')` lists training circuits only).
- **Source scan:** the allowlists above.
- **Page wiring (Node, fakes) and E2E (Chromium and WebKit, desktop, phone and tablet):** choose the second training circuit, run a few generations, see its name and track drawn; the selector is disabled mid-run; a saved model records the circuit; resuming a checkpoint on another circuit is refused with a message; the layout checks (44 px control, no horizontal scroll at 320 px) still pass.
- **Mutation checks:** weakening each enforcement test's subject (for example removing a split check or adding a held-out ID to the training list) fails a test.
- Not tested here: any model on the new validation or held-out circuits, and any physical device.

## 7. Owner checks (iPhone and iPad, before a completion request)

1. In `?mode=train`, open the **Training circuit** choice, pick the second circuit, run a few generations: the track drawn matches the choice and the page stays usable in portrait and landscape.
2. Save a champion, reload and replay it: it replays on its own circuit; the saved-champions list names the circuit.
3. The new control is easy to tap (44 px) and nothing is clipped.

## 8. Limits

Circuits are hand-specified polylines in a fixed format (no varying width, no pit lanes, one driving direction), so "varied corner geometry" means varied within that family. Four new circuits cannot cover track design; P4-06 adds a real-world circuit and P4-03 custom ones. The split protocol is only as strong as the discipline of not looking: the allowlist test and the provenance make a breach visible, not impossible. ST1 to ST5, every existing study seed and record, and the P5-01 protocol's held-out opening rule are untouched.
